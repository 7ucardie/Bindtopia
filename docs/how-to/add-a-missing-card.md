# Add a card that is not in the catalog

Bindtopia's catalog comes from TCGdex. When a card is missing there (a rare promo, a misprint, a regional release), you can still keep it and trade it.

## In your collection

1. Open **Saved → + Add a card yourself**, or search for the card and choose **Card not listed? Add it yourself** when nothing is found (the name you searched for is filled in).
2. Enter the name. Set, card number, card language, your own price (in your market's currency) and a note are optional.
3. Choose **Save**. The card appears in **Saved** with the label *Own entry*.

The card exists only in this browser, like the rest of your collection. It goes into backups (version 2, with `"custom": true`) and comes back on import. Deleting it asks for confirmation, because there is no catalog copy to restore it from. Backups with own entries cannot be read by versions of Bindtopia from before this feature.

## In trade mode

Choose **Card not listed? Add it by hand** under the search field, enter the name and optionally the price you agreed, then **+ I get** or **+ I give**. A card with a price counts in the totals; without a price it is listed but not counted.

## Report it to TCGdex

Opening an own entry shows **Report this card to TCGdex ↗**. It opens a prefilled issue in the open [TCGdex card database](https://github.com/tcgdex/cards-database) with only the card's name, set, number and language, never your note or price. Nothing is posted until you submit it with your own GitHub account. Once TCGdex adds the card, Bindtopia's daily update brings it into the catalog for everyone.

Own entries are not checked by anyone: they are not evidence of a card's existence, authenticity or value.
